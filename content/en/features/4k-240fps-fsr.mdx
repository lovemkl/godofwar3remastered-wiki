The words “4K” and “240 FPS” can describe selectable options rather than a sustained result. This wiki has not independently measured either. The older page presented unsupported hardware claims as tested recommendations; this revision separates configuration from evidence.

## Resolution and Frame Rate Are Different Measurements

Output resolution describes the image size. Measured frame rate describes how often a frame is delivered under a particular workload. Selecting both high resolution and a high cap does not prove the system sustains that combination. Record the game scene, settings and hardware before comparing reports.

## Check FSR Against the Release

The [maintainer’s feature-availability table](https://github.com/FelipePrado0/SpartanPort#feature-availability) marks FSR options disabled in the versions documented there. This corrects our older blanket recommendation to enable FSR. Follow the table and notes for the exact package you use; do not infer availability from an early press headline.

## Where to Find Evidence

[Version 2.7.0 release notes](https://github.com/FelipePrado0/SpartanPort/releases/tag/windows-v2.7.0) describe specific test configurations and their limitations. Those are maintainer/player results. We have not independently reproduced them or completed a campaign run.
